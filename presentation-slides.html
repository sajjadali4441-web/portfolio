SECTION 04 // SPATIAL ENGINE

100m Geofence: Haversine Algorithm

Zero third-party commercial mapping dependencies. Mathematically verified on client and server.

The Spherical Geodesic Formula

Δσ = 2 · arcsin(√(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁)·cos(φ₂)·sin²(Δλ/2)))

d = R · Δσ (R = 6,371,000 meters)
  • Sub-Meter Precision: Accurately measures physical distance from office coordinates.
  • Allowed Boundary: Strict ≤ 100 meters threshold for punches.
